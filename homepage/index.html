About

Why this exists

Social media acts as a host to an unprecedented rate of decontextualised images where the lines between fact and fiction are distorted, often underscored by motivations to push messages into the public consciousness in a targeted way.

Born out of an urgent concern for how technology intersects with an increasingly dangerous and polarising sociopolitical climate, this taxonomy to read audiovisual content on social media sites has intentions of restoring the public's power through improving political literacy.

Due to the rapidly evolving nature of social media platforms and crosscultural technologies we emphasise this to be an ongoing pursuit of awareness of the new languages driving both mundane, insidious propaganda as well as overt extremism, so we hope that these image analyses and external resources empower the viewer to read further into the potent narratives constructed within a mindless scroll, to inform how we all act in the real world.
